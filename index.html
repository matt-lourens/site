Hey, I'm Matt. I invent and use computational tools to better understand quantum many-body systems. My background is in machine learning and physics. I obtained an MSc in data science from North-West University and then worked for about two years at Capitec Bank as a machine learning engineer. I wanted to learn more about the physical phenomena surrounding me, and pursued a PhD in physics at Stellenbosch University (defended successfully in 2026). I still want to learn about physical phenomena, so I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Stellenbosch University hosted by Prof. Francesco Petruccione.
