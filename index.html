Hey, I'm Matt. I invent and use computational tools to better understand quantum many-body systems. My background used to be in Machine Learning, I obtained a Masters of Science degree from North-West University in data science and then worked for about two years at Capitec bank as a Machine Learning Engineer. I wanted to learn more about the physical phenomena surrounding me, and pursued a PhD in Physics at Stellenbosch University (defended successfully in 2026). I still want to learn more about my physical surroundings so I'm pursuing a career in research. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Stellenbosch University hosted by Prof. Francesco Petruccione.
